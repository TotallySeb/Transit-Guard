# LA Metro Rules and Regulations

This document outlines the main rules and guidelines riders must follow when using LA Metro’s public transportation services, including buses, light rail, and subway.

---

## 1. Fare Payment

- All riders must **pay the appropriate fare before boarding** or upon entering the fare-paid zone.
- Accepted payment methods include:
  - TAP Card (contactless smart card)
  - Mobile ticketing via official apps
  - Cash (exact fare only on buses)
- **Fare evasion is prohibited** and may result in fines or penalties.

---

## 2. Boarding and Riding

- Allow passengers to exit before boarding.
- Give priority seating to seniors, people with disabilities, and pregnant women.
- **No eating, drinking, or smoking** on buses or trains.
- Use headphones when listening to audio devices to avoid disturbing others.
- Keep aisles and doorways clear for safety.
- Do not block seats or stand in restricted areas.

---

## 3. Safety and Conduct

- Follow all posted safety signs and instructions from Metro staff.
- No weapons, illegal substances, or hazardous materials allowed.
- No vandalism, graffiti, or destruction of Metro property.
- Harassment, fighting, or disruptive behavior is strictly prohibited.
- Report suspicious activities or emergencies to Metro personnel or law enforcement immediately.

---

## 4. Pets and Service Animals

- Service animals assisting riders with disabilities are allowed.
- Small pets must be in carriers and kept off seats.
- Animals should not disturb or threaten other passengers.

---

## 5. Accessibility

- Metro provides accessible services for riders with disabilities.
- Priority seating and accessible boarding areas are available.
- Assistance is provided upon request when possible.

---

## 6. Bicycles and Personal Mobility Devices

- Bicycles are allowed on buses with bike racks (first-come, first-served).
- Folding bikes can be brought onboard if folded.
- Personal mobility devices (e.g., scooters, wheelchairs) are allowed if they meet size and safety requirements.

---

## 7. Lost and Found

- Items left on Metro property can be reported or claimed through the Lost and Found office.
- Metro is not responsible for lost or stolen personal belongings.

---

## 8. Service Interruptions and Delays

- Riders should check service alerts and advisories before traveling.
- Follow alternative routes or instructions provided by Metro during disruptions.

---

## 9. Enforcement and Penalties

- Transit enforcement officers may conduct fare inspections and enforce rules.
- Violations can result in fines, citations, or removal from the system.
- Serious offenses may be referred to law enforcement.
