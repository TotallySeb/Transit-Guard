# Safety Concerns on the Metro Expo Line (E Line)

The Expo Line (now the E Line) is a light rail route that connects **Downtown Los Angeles** with **Santa Monica**, running through key neighborhoods like **USC**, **Crenshaw**, **Culver City**, and **West LA**. Once marketed as a clean, beach-to-city alternative, the line has faced **increased scrutiny** over the last few years due to rising safety concerns, especially during off-peak hours.

---

## 🚨 Key Safety Issues

### 1. Crime in Transit-Adjacent Neighborhoods
- Stations like **Expo/Western**, **Expo/Crenshaw**, and **LATTC/Ortho Institute** are located in high-crime corridors.
- Riders report **frequent assaults, harassment, and phone snatching**, particularly during early mornings and late evenings.
- The proximity to **freeways, empty lots, and underpasses** makes several stations feel unsafe.

### 2. Lack of Uniformed Presence
- There is a noticeable **lack of visible Metro security** or LAPD officers along much of the Expo Line.
- Offenders often board without fare and behave aggressively without intervention.
- Emergency intercoms are reportedly unresponsive or ignored, leading many riders to **avoid reporting incidents**.

### 3. Open Drug Use and Loitering
- **Open-air drug use** is common at stations like **La Cienega/Jefferson**, **Expo/Crenshaw**, and **USC/Expo Park**.
- Some stations have become **gathering spots for unhoused individuals**, especially during extreme weather.
- Riders frequently encounter **smoke, needles, or trash** inside train cars and elevators.

### 4. Isolated Westside Stations at Night
- Though Westside stations like **Downtown Santa Monica**, **26th St/Bergamot**, and **Expo/Bundy** are well-lit, they’re often **deserted at night**.
- Riders report feeling vulnerable due to **empty platforms, long waits**, and **poor cell service** in parts of the route.
- Auto break-ins in park-and-ride lots are also common complaints.

## 📉 Impact on Ridership and Perception

- Despite connecting major attractions like **USC**, **Staples Center**, and **Santa Monica Pier**, safety complaints have led to a **drop in ridership** during non-commute hours.
- Tourists often report **surprise at the conditions**, citing a disconnect between the city's image and the transit experience.
- The **contrast between upscale Westside stops and under-resourced South LA stations** highlights systemic inequalities in the transit experience.

---

## 🛠 Suggested Improvements

- Deploy **uniformed Metro Ambassadors** and **crisis-trained security staff** at key trouble spots.
- Enhance **station lighting, cleanliness, and visibility**, especially at transfer hubs.
- Add **real-time surveillance** and **automated alerts** for drug use and loitering detection.
- Partner with community organizations to offer **support services** near high-risk stations.
