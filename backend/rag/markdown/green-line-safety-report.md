# Safety Concerns on the Metro Green Line (C Line)

The Metro Green Line, rebranded as the C Line, connects Redondo Beach to Norwalk via the 105 Freeway median. Although less publicized than the Red or Blue Lines, the Green Line also faces a range of safety concerns, especially regarding **isolation, security staffing, and accessibility**.

---

## 🚨 Key Safety Concerns

### 1. Isolated Station Design
- Many Green Line stations are built **in the middle of freeways or industrial areas**, leaving riders feeling isolated.
- Stations such as **Hawthorne/Lennox**, **Lakewood Blvd**, and **Long Beach Blvd** have **limited pedestrian access** and poor lighting.
- Riders have reported feeling unsafe due to a lack of **public visibility or foot traffic**, particularly at night.

### 2. Minimal Security Presence
- Unlike higher-traffic lines, the Green Line often lacks consistent **Metro security or law enforcement patrols**.
- Emergency intercoms and cameras exist, but riders say **responses are slow or non-existent**.
- Reports of harassment, drug use, and loitering often go **unaddressed for hours**.

### 3. Substance Use and Vandalism
- Open drug use occurs on platforms and in elevator shafts.
- Some stations (e.g., **Crenshaw**, **Avalon**) are known for **syringes, human waste**, and vandalized ticketing machines.
- Elevators and escalators are often broken or filthy, impacting **ADA access and rider safety**.

### 4. Poor Lighting and Surveillance
- Several stations suffer from **inadequate lighting**, especially at exits or in stairwells.
- CCTV cameras are outdated or poorly positioned, and many riders are unaware they exist at all.
- These conditions increase the risk of **theft, assault, and unreported incidents**.

---

## 📉 Operational Challenges

- The Green Line has **fewer riders** than other lines, but disproportionately high safety complaints per capita.
- Its **isolation and lack of connecting bus service** leave riders with few safe alternatives.
- Women and elderly riders cite it as one of the **least safe lines** in the Metro system.

---

## 🛠 Suggested Safety Improvements

- Add **uniformed security patrols** to platforms, especially during off-peak hours.
- Improve **lighting, cleanliness, and signage** to increase station visibility and wayfinding.
- Redesign or enhance access pathways for **greater public visibility** and safer pedestrian zones.
- Invest in **real-time monitoring systems** and **community-based reporting tools**.
