# Metro Rail Lines Overview

The Los Angeles Metro Rail system consists of several distinct rail lines serving the Greater Los Angeles area. Each line has unique routes, service characteristics, and connections, providing comprehensive transit coverage.

---

## Current Metro Rail Lines

| Line | Color        | Type         | Route Highlights                                  |
|-------|--------------|--------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| **A Line** | Blue         | Light Rail   | Connects Downtown LA to Long Beach, passing through South LA and Watts. One of the busiest lines. |
| **B Line** | Red          | Heavy Rail   | Runs from Union Station through Downtown LA and Koreatown to North Hollywood. Provides rapid subway service. |
| **C Line** | Green        | Light Rail   | East-West line serving South Bay, connecting Redondo Beach and Norwalk (planned extension). Access to LAX area. |
| **D Line** | Purple       | Heavy Rail   | Subway line running from Downtown LA to Koreatown, currently under extension toward Westwood/UCLA. |
| **E Line** | Expo (Light Rail) | Light Rail   | Connects Downtown LA with Santa Monica via Expo Park and Culver City. Popular for westside commuters and tourists. |
| **K Line** | Crenshaw/LAX | Light Rail   | Runs from Expo/Crenshaw to LAX/Metro Transit Center, connecting South LA and Inglewood. Expanding service planned. |
| **L Line** | Gold         | Light Rail   | Currently partially inactive due to construction; traditionally ran from East LA to Azusa, undergoing reconfiguration. |

---

## Key Characteristics

- **Heavy Rail Lines (B & D):** Fully underground subway service with higher speeds and fewer stops.
- **Light Rail Lines (A, C, E, K, L):** Mix of surface-level, elevated, and underground segments; serve a broader set of neighborhoods.
- **Operating Hours:** Most lines run from approximately 4:00 AM to 12:30 AM daily.
- **Fare Integration:** All lines use the TAP card system for fare payment and transfers.

---

## Major Transfer Stations

- **Union Station:** Central hub connecting A, B, D Lines and regional rail services.
- **7th St/Metro Center:** Transfers between A, B, D, and E Lines.
- **Pico Station:** Transfer point between A and E Lines.
- **Wilshire/Vermont:** Transfers between B and D Lines.
- **Expo/Crenshaw:** Transfer point between E and K Lines.

---

## Ridership and Service Notes

- The **A Line** and **B Line** are among the system’s highest-ridership lines.
- The **D Line** extension is anticipated to increase ridership significantly when completed.
- The **E Line** serves key destinations such as USC, Downtown Santa Monica, and Culver City.
- The **K Line** enhances service to growing South LA and LAX areas.
