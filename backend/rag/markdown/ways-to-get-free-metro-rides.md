# Legal Ways to Get Free or Reduced Metro Rides in Los Angeles

Los Angeles Metro offers several programs and initiatives that provide **free or discounted transit rides** to eligible riders. This guide explains the main ways users can access fare-free or reduced-fare Metro rides legally.

---

## 1. Fare-Free Programs for Youth and Students

### Metro Youth Program
- **Who Qualifies:** Youth aged 18 and under.
- **What It Offers:** Free rides on all Metro buses and trains.
- **How to Access:** Youth simply tap a **Youth TAP card** loaded through participating schools or community organizations.
- **Where to Learn More:** [Metro Youth TAP Program](https://www.metro.net/riding/fares/youth-fare/)

### Free Transit for Students (Under 25)
- **Who Qualifies:** College and high school students under 25.
- **Program Details:** Metro is expanding free transit access for students across LA County.
- **Enrollment:** Through school partnerships or via Metro’s TAP customer service.
  
---

## 2. Low-Income Fare Programs

### Reduced Fare TAP Card
- **Who Qualifies:** Riders with income at or below 250% of the federal poverty level.
- **Benefits:** 50% off all Metro fares on buses and trains.
- **How to Apply:** Submit proof of income and fill out application forms online or at Metro Customer Centers.
- **More Info:** [Reduced Fare Program](https://www.metro.net/riding/fares/reduced-fare/)

### Regional Transit Connections (RTC) Card
- **What It Is:** A fare discount program accepted by Metro and other regional transit agencies.
- **Who Qualifies:** Low-income riders, people with disabilities, and seniors.
- **How to Get It:** Apply through local social service agencies or Metro offices.

---

## 3. Special Programs for Seniors and People with Disabilities

### Senior TAP Card
- **Who Qualifies:** Riders aged 62 and older.
- **Benefits:** Half-price fares on Metro.
- **How to Apply:** Provide proof of age at Metro Customer Centers.

### Disabled TAP Card
- **Who Qualifies:** Riders with disabilities who have certification.
- **Benefits:** 50% fare discount.
- **More Info:** [Disabled Rider Program](https://www.metro.net/riding/fares/disabled-fare/)

---

## 4. Temporary and Event-Based Free Rides

### Fare-Free Days
- Metro occasionally offers **fare-free service** on special occasions, holidays, or during major events.
- Check Metro’s official website and social media for announcements.

### Emergency or Disaster Response
- In case of natural disasters or emergencies, Metro may offer **free transit services** for affected residents.

---

## 5. Other Tips to Ride Free or Save Money Legally

- **Promotional Passes:** Some employers and universities provide **free or discounted transit passes**.
- **Metro Micro & Shuttle Services:** Certain local shuttle services may be free or low-cost.
- **TAP Card Auto-Reload & Fare Capping:** Pay-as-you-go riders benefit from **daily and weekly fare caps**, limiting how much you spend.
