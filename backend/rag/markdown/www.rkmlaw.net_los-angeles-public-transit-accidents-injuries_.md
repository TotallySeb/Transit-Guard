In 2024, LA Metro buses experienced more crime-related incidents (10) than LA Metro trains (6), with stabbings and shootings being the primary causes of injuries. However, train accidents resulted in more severe injuries, with one notable train collision in April 2024 which resulted in 14 people hospitalized and 55 injured. In 2024, LA Metro buses had more reported accidents overall (6 bus accidents vs. 3 train accidents), and also had a higher number of hospitalizations per incident than train accidents. Out of 6 reported bus crashes in Los Angeles in 2024, there were 18 hospitalizations and one death of a pedestrian who was fatally struck in the City of Industry.
