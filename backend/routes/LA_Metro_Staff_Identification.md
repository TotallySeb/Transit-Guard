# How to Identify LA Metro Staff and Officials by Appearance

When using LA Metro services, you may encounter various employees and officials responsible for operations, safety, and customer service. Knowing how to recognize their roles by appearance can help you get assistance quickly and understand who to approach.

---

## 1. Transit Operators (Bus and Rail Drivers)

- **Uniform:** Typically wear navy blue or dark blue uniforms with LA Metro patches or logos.
- **Badge:** Company-issued ID badge often worn on a lanyard or clipped to the shirt.
- **Location:** Seen operating buses or trains; usually inside the driver’s cabin.
- **Role:** Responsible for safely operating the vehicle and assisting passengers.

---

## 2. Fare Enforcement Officers (Transit Security Officers)

- **Uniform:** Dark blue or black uniforms resembling law enforcement attire, often with "Metro Transit Enforcement" patches.
- **Badge & Equipment:** Wears official badges, radios, and sometimes body cameras. May carry handcuffs.
- **Presence:** Patrol stations, vehicles, and platforms to check fares and enforce rules.
- **Role:** Enforce fare payment, ensure safety, and respond to incidents.

---

## 3. Station Attendants and Customer Service Representatives

- **Uniform:** May wear branded polo shirts, vests, or jackets with the Metro logo.
- **Badge:** Visible ID badges.
- **Location:** Staff ticket booths, platforms, or customer service centers.
- **Role:** Provide information, help with ticketing, and assist passengers.

---

## 4. Maintenance and Engineering Staff

- **Uniform:** Usually wear safety vests, hard hats, and work uniforms with reflective stripes and Metro insignia.
- **Equipment:** May carry tools and wear protective gear on worksites.
- **Role:** Maintain infrastructure, vehicles, and ensure system functionality.

---

## 5. Executive and Administrative Staff

- **Appearance:** Typically business attire, often not wearing uniforms.
- **Badges:** May have official Metro ID badges.
- **Location:** Usually found in Metro offices or attending public meetings.
- **Role:** Manage operations, planning, and administration.

---

## 6. Police Officers (LA Metro Transit Police Department)

- **Uniform:** Standard police uniforms with patches identifying them as Metro Transit Police.
- **Badge and Gear:** Police badges, firearms, radios, and other law enforcement equipment.
- **Role:** Provide law enforcement and security on Metro property.

---

## Tips for Identification

- **Look for the Metro Logo:** Most staff uniforms and badges display the official LA Metro logo.
- **Ask Politely:** If unsure, approach staff and ask for assistance.
- **Emergency Contacts:** Use station intercoms or customer service phones if you need help but cannot locate staff.
